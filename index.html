Step 2 of 3

About this journal

What this page is for

This journal webpage was created as part of a screen time intervention study, meant to provide you a space where you can occasionally reflect on your smartphone use.

What can the researcher see?

The researcher will be able to see your user number, whether you finished setting up the journal, the Monday your journal week started, and on how many of the 7 days you opened it (a number from 0 to 7). This is compared with the Screen Time you report in Qualtrics.

The research team will not be able to see what you write or choose here: your dashboard, intentions, reasons, reflections and settings. They are locked with your passcode, so nobody else can read them, not even the research team.

How long is my journal kept?

Your journal is kept until 26 January 2027, and is then permanently deleted.

Every question in this journal is optional, and you can skip any of them. Remember, you can reach out to the researcher if you have questions about the study or if you want to withdraw from it.
